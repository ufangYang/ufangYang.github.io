About this project

Neyeronstein is a side project, not peer-reviewed research. It started from curiosity, a place where my day job (EEG, eye-tracking, and how the brain reads faces and gaze) meets my love of signal processing, visualisation and psychology.

I built it to make brain signals something you can see and feel. The visuals are art driven by live EEG, not a diagnostic, a therapy, or a health claim.
